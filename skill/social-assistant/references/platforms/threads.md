# Threads (Instagram)

## How reach works (as commonly observed; it changes, so check memory/LEARNINGS.md)

- **Conversation beats broadcasting.** Posts that ask something or share a relatable
  moment get more replies, and replies drive reach.
- **Links reduce reach** like on X. Share the tip in the post, and add the link in a reply
  to your own post or keep it in your bio.
- **One topic tag per post** (e.g. "Mac", "Apple", "Tech Tips") puts it in front of people
  following that topic. Pick the most specific one that fits.
- 500 characters per post. Multi-post threads work for step-by-step content.
- Tone is warmer and more personal than X. Less snark, more "this annoyed me, here's what fixed it".

## Formats

**Relatable tip (the default):**
```
<a feeling or moment people recognize>

<the fix, 1–3 lines>

<a question back to readers>
```
Example:
```
That moment when your Mac says "disk almost full" and you've barely installed anything 🙃

Nine times out of ten it's Time Machine snapshots hiding in System Data. Terminal:
tmutil listlocalsnapshots /

What's the weirdest thing you've found eating your storage?
```

**Behind the scenes (maker posts, about 1 in 4):** what you're building, a real
number (visitors, downloads, a bug you fixed), what you learned. People follow makers who share the journey.

**Reply to others:** friendly, specific, 1–2 lines. No link unless asked.

## Style

- Conversational, first person, an emoji now and then is fine here.
- End with a real question more often than on X.
- Never post the exact same text as X on the same day. Rewrite for the tone.
